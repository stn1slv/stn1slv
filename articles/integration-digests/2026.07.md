🔍 [Domain Driven APIs — Part 2: From DTOs to DSLs (When Your API Consumer is an AI Agent)](https://medium.com/@shalin.garg/domain-driven-apis-part-2-from-dtos-to-dsls-when-your-api-consumer-is-an-ai-agent-95de1d1c33cc)

🔍 [Partial Failure in Fan-Out Systems: When Three Destinations Succeed and Two Fail](https://meshes.io/blog/partial-failure-fan-out-systems)

_Distinctive contribution: it operationalizes partial-failure recovery in fan-out by separating parent event rollup from per-destination delivery records. Failures are classified into retryable, permanent, and ambiguous; ambiguous timeouts trigger idempotent reattempt semantics, while permanent rejects pause until payload/mapping fixes, avoiding replays that duplicate successful destinations._
